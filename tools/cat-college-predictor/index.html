CAT 2026 Slot Normalization: How Scaled Scores Are Calculated

Because CAT is administered across three separate test sessions—Slot 1 (Morning: 8:30 AM - 10:30 AM), Slot 2 (Afternoon: 12:30 PM - 2:30 PM), and Slot 3 (Evening: 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM)—variations in question difficulty inevitably occur. To establish complete parity, the Indian Institutes of Management employ the equipercentile equating statistical normalization model.

Scaled Score = M_g + ( (S_i - M_i) / SD_i ) × SD_g
Where: S_i = Candidate Raw Score, M_i = Slot Mean, SD_i = Slot Std Deviation, M_g = Master Global Mean, SD_g = Master Global Std Deviation

Session-Wise Mean Calculation

Mean and standard deviation of raw marks for top 0.1% test-takers and total test-takers are computed independently for Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3.

Equating Standard Deviations

If Slot 2 featured a tougher QA section with lower average attempts, candidate scores in that slot receive an upward adjustment to match global distributions.

Sectional Scaling Independent

Scaling is executed independently for VARC, DILR, and QA. Your total scaled score is the mathematical sum of your three normalized sectional scaled scores.

Percentile Rank Generation

Calculated as P = ( (N - R) / N ) × 100, where N is total test-takers across India (~2.9 Lakhs) and R is the candidate's national rank.

Beyond CAT Percentile: How B-Schools Calculate Composite Scores

Securing a 99+ percentile in CAT guarantees eligibility for shortlists, but final admission offers depend on your cumulative composite score. Leading institutions allocate weightage across the following key dimensions:

CAT Scaled Score (40% - 60%)

Sectional performance in VARC, DILR, and QA ensures candidates demonstrate well-rounded analytical and linguistic competencies.

Academic Record (15% - 25%)

Class 10th and 12th board marks, alongside undergraduate percentage/CGPA, reflect sustained academic consistency over time.

Gender & Academic Diversity (5% - 10%)

Additional points are awarded to female/transgender applicants and non-engineering graduates (Commerce, Arts, Science, Medicine, Law).

Work Experience (5% - 10%)

Quality full-time executive experience post-graduation (optimally between 24 and 36 months) is highly rewarded across top IIMs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the CAT College Predictor 2026 work?
The FindMyBSchool CAT College Predictor evaluates your sectional raw marks (VARC, DILR, QA) or official response sheet Digialm URL, applies slot normalization across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3, and generates your estimated national percentile. It then cross-references your projected percentile with verified category-wise cutoffs for 21 IIMs and over 50 top-tier MBA/PGDM colleges.
What CAT percentile is required for IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta?
For General Category engineering candidates, top IIMs typically require a minimum 99.2 to 99.8 percentile in CAT, alongside strong sectional scores (usually 80-85+ sectional percentile). Non-engineers with exceptional academic records (90%+ in 10th/12th/Graduation) and NC-OBC/SC/ST/EWS candidates may receive interview calls between 90 and 96 percentile.
Which top MBA/PGDM colleges accept 80 to 90 CAT percentile?
With an 80 to 90 CAT percentile, candidates have strong shortlist prospects at reputed business schools including GIM Goa, FORE School of Management Delhi, Great Lakes Institute of Management Chennai (PGDM), TAPMI Manipal, BIMTECH Greater Noida, LIBA Chennai, and KJ Somaiya Mumbai.
Can I get an IIM interview call with an 85 to 90 percentile in CAT?
Yes. While General Category engineering candidates need 98+ percentile, candidates belonging to NC-OBC, EWS, SC, ST, and PwD categories frequently receive shortlist calls from Baby and New IIMs (such as IIM Bodhgaya, IIM Sambalpur, IIM Sirmaur, IIM Jammu, IIM Nagpur, and IIM Amritsar) in the 80 to 90 percentile range, provided minimum sectional cutoffs (typically 65-75%ile) are satisfied.
How does CAT slot normalization affect raw scores across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3?
Because CAT is conducted across 3 separate slots with varying question difficulty, IIMs employ an equipercentile statistical normalization model. If your slot was comparatively harder, your scaled score is adjusted upward; if your slot had a higher average, the score adjusts slightly downward to ensure complete fairness across morning, afternoon, and evening slots.
Which premier non-IIM business schools accept CAT scores?
Premier non-IIM institutions accepting CAT scores include FMS Delhi (Faculty of Management Studies), SPJIMR Mumbai, MDI Gurgaon, IIT Bombay (SJMSOM), IIT Delhi (DMS), IIT Kharagpur (VGSOM), IIFT Delhi, IMI New Delhi, and IMT Ghaziabad.
What is the official CAT marking scheme for MCQs and non-MCQs (TITA)?
In CAT 2026, correct Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) award +3 marks, while incorrect MCQs penalize -1 mark. Non-MCQ / TITA (Type In The Answer) questions award +3 marks for a correct response, but carry ZERO negative deduction (0 penalty) for incorrect responses.
What is the difference between CAT raw score, scaled score, and percentile?
Your Raw Score is your direct points total from +3/-1 marking out of 198. The Scaled Score is your score adjusted for slot difficulty through equipercentile equating. Your Percentile represents the percentage of all national test-takers who scored equal to or less than your scaled score.
How do top IIMs calculate the Composite Score for final admissions?
Final admission offers are based on a Composite Score, not percentile alone. Top IIMs weight CAT performance (40-60%), 10th and 12th board marks (10-20%), undergraduate degree (5-15%), relevant work experience (5-10%), gender/academic diversity (5-10%), and Personal Interview / WAT performance (30-40%).
