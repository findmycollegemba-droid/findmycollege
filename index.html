Frequently Asked Questions (Admissions & Cutoffs)

Q: What is the main difference between an MBA and a PGDM degree in India?
A: An MBA is a master's degree granted by university-affiliated institutions (e.g., FMS Delhi, JBIMS Mumbai, PUMBA) under UGC. A PGDM is an autonomous diploma awarded by industry-focused institutions (e.g., IIMs, XLRI, SPJIMR, MDI) approved by AICTE. Both are treated as fully equivalent in corporate hiring and government recruitments when AIU accredited.

Q: Which MBA colleges in India provide the highest Return on Investment (ROI)?
A: The highest ROI business schools in India are FMS Delhi (2-year fees ~₹2.0 Lakhs with average CTC of ₹34.1 LPA), JBIMS Mumbai (fees ~₹6.0 Lakhs with average CTC of ₹28.0 LPA), and TISS Mumbai (fees ~₹2.5 Lakhs with average CTC of ₹27.2 LPA).

Q: How does the CAT slot normalization work across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3?
A: CAT conducts slot normalization using the equipercentile equating method to adjust for differences in section difficulty. Raw scores are converted to scaled scores based on percentile equivalence across morning, afternoon, and evening test slots.
